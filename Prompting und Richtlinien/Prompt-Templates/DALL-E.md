# Stil für alle Blog-Artikel Bilder

```
A minimalist stick figure illustration on a pure white background with generous negative space (about 30 to 40 percent of the canvas remains empty). Lines are crisp, confident, and continuous, drawn in warm gray (`#5A5856`). No shading, no gradients, no photographic realism, no facial details. Soft watercolor washes serve as sparing accent color in muted blue (`#9FBFE9`), warm orange (`#EBC49F`), and soft green (`#AAC8A7`), with transparency around 25 to 35 percent so the underlying white remains visible. The watercolor is loose and organic, never fully filling shapes. Style: hand-drawn, calm, systemic, contemplative. Aspect ratio 16:9, 1280×720 px.
```


# Bildbeschreibungen (Blog) als Beispiel

```
A single stick figure stands in a calm, contemplative posture on the left half of the composition, slightly turned toward the right, one hand near the chin in a gesture of quiet reflection. The figure faces a clean 3-by-3 grid that occupies the right half of the canvas, sketched in the same warm gray lines as if drawn in the air or lightly projected, with thin and steady strokes. Each of the nine cells is gently touched by a small, irregular watercolor wash: three or four cells in muted blue, two or three in warm orange, one or two in soft green, while two cells remain almost empty. The colors are asymmetrical, do not align in obvious patterns, and never fill a cell completely, suggesting that each field carries its own qualitative depth. The figure stands at a respectful distance from the grid, neither pointing nor controlling it. Soft, generous white space surrounds both elements, especially in the lower third and upper right of the canvas. The overall mood is quiet, attentive, methodically curious.
```
