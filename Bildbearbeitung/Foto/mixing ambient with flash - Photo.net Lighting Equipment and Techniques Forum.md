A lot of books advise to meter the ambient, and then match the flash output (metering incident flash on the subject) until it gives that same aperture, so the exposure matches. They also advise to use as slow a shutterspeed as possible, so that an as small as possible aperture can be used, so that the flash output can be low to match the low ambient. (Flash output sometimes cannot be set low enough) Ambient can then still be controlled by changing the shutterspeed
