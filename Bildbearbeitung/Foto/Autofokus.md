Viewfinder **face detection** on the D750/D810 works as follows:
1) Set **Matrix Metring**; Turn VF face detection on in the menu under Matrix Metering (custom setting **b4**)
2) Put the camera in Auto Area AF mode (Not to be confused with AF-A which simply chooses between **AF-S and AF-C** for you)
Viewfinder Face detection works in AF-A, AF-S and AF-C as long as **Auto Area** is selected, or in **Group AF when used with AF-S**.

Auto Area - AF-S und AF-C
Group AF - AF-S

**Group AF** is a **closest subject** priority mode, and when used in **AF-S it prioritizes faces when VF Face Detection and matrix metering are enabled.**

Viewfinder face detection on the D750/D810 works as follows:
1) Turn VF face detection on in the menu under Matrix Metering - **custom setting B4 on the D750 and B5 on the D810**
2) Put the camera in Auto **Area AF** mode (Not to be confused with AF-A which simply chooses between AF-S and AF-C for you)

Viewfinder Face detection works in AF-A, AF-S and AF-C as long as Auto Area is selected, or in **Group AF when used with AF-S**.
**Group AF is a closest subject priority mode**, and when used in AF-S it prioritizes faces when VF Face Detection and matrix metering are enabled.
